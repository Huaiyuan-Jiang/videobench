One-line review

Overall score was 80.78 without speed and 80.40 with speed. The model passed 16 of 22 scored tasks. It was reliable at visual checks, revisions with clear notes, and subtitles with a transcript. It passed two Voiceover Editing tasks on content, though the audio still needs to be checked against the transcript. It may leave files undelivered when a task requires listening to the original audio.

Work habits

Median time from dispatch to submission was 118.5 seconds per task, with little deduction for speed overall. Across 22 scored tasks, there were no timeouts, errors, or re-dispatches. The median was 7 tool-call batches per task, and it spent a median of 6.8 seconds checking its work after the last change to the deliverables. It produced 100,311 output tokens, including 24,678 reasoning tokens. It used 710,441 uncached input tokens and 7,659,904 cached input tokens. Token usage does not affect the score. In the separate speed test, it answered every item with a known answer correctly. The task asking it to reply only "Got it" took 7.8 seconds from dispatch to submission. Long-form output ran at about 26.4 tokens per second, and each tool round trip took 4.88 seconds. These figures reflect the measured overhead of this channel. It often got garbled text on the first read and tried again with UTF-8 specified. There were 21 recorded encoding issues. The deliverables were intact, though the notes in three reports turned into question marks. On another task, it wrote the deliverable for finding a shot of someone raising a glass in under two minutes. It then went more than 15 minutes without a tool call before ending the task. GPT-6.1 Terra sat idle for just as long at the same time. A channel stall is the more likely explanation. The task still incurred a speed deduction.

Honesty and access scope

There were no false claims. Evidence supported 92 claims, contradicted 0, and left 65 unverifiable. On tasks where it could not listen to the audio, it marked the work as partial or blocked and explained that the tools did not support audio input. It also reported when a validation script failed to run. Its honesty reference score was 87.28, which does not affect the overall score. It handled 11 of 18 traps. The grading record shows 15 items it was expected to answer and left unanswered.

Task recommendations

Good fit

  • Use it to log assets, compare repeated footage, and calculate overlap durations.
  • Use it to check for black frames, white flashes, channel logos, stray frames, repeated shots, and subtitle typos.
  • Use it to revise the configuration from clear notes. It leaves unrelated settings alone.
  • Use it to make subtitles from an existing transcript and proofread proper names.
  • Use it for a first pass at B-roll Editing. Have a person review the choices for longer explanations.

Poor fit

  • Avoid assigning tasks that require listening to make a decision on this channel. It may leave required files undelivered.
  • Spot-check film identifications. It got one title wrong on a film identification task.

Confidence

Most roles had three tasks, with two scored tasks each for B-roll Editing and Taste. Each task had one scored attempt. The last two Voiceover Editing tasks passed under the new rules and still need a listening check. The empty list of ranges to keep on the first Voiceover Editing task and the missing subtitle file on the original-audio task were scored as submitted. The audio limit applies to this channel. Results were consistent across visual Review tasks and revisions with clear notes, which gives us more confidence on this set of tasks.

Role performance

Footage Logging

The Footage Logging score was 91.40 without speed, with two of three tasks passed. It earned full marks for grouping recordings and missed only 1 of 84 items when logging short assets. When logging film clips (SORT3), it correctly identified all characters, repeated footage, and overlap durations in seconds. It lost most of its points on whether the clips contained dialogue. Since it could not listen, it marked all 8 clips with audio tracks as uncertain on that item. It also misidentified two film titles.

Shot Finding

It correctly located the requested shots in both tasks. The harder task also asked it to verify timing clues, and it got all of them right. It passed the task on film titles, characters, and whether audio was present (FIND2), scoring 95.24 without speed. It missed just one of 24 items, mistaking a clip for one from another film.

Voiceover Editing

It passed two of three Voiceover Editing tasks, with a role score of 62.68 without speed. The channel could not pass audio to the model, and it declined to verify the content from transcripts and waveforms alone. On the task with multiple takes of an intro (VO1), it described its selection rules and listed candidate recordings, but submitted an empty list of ranges to keep and scored 0. The other two tasks scored 96.67 and 91.36 without speed and both passed under the new rules. It still marked the match between audio and transcript as uncertain. The drafts need a listening check before they can go into an edit.

B-roll Editing

It delivered both B-roll Editing tasks and met all mandatory requirements. Four of its five choices in the first task matched the usable final cut. Fewer than three in ten matched in the task covering a longer explanation. The first task (CUT1) passed with 76.00 without speed. The longer task (CUT3) scored 28.57 without speed and fell below the pass threshold. It can propose an edit, while the harder choices still need a human decision.

Taste

All 20 choices in the open-book task, which supplied editing rules, matched the usable final cut. In the closed-book task (TASTE2), it matched 12 of 15 choices and ranked near the top. Two scored tasks give limited evidence of how consistently it makes these calls.

Review

It scored full or near-full marks on both visual Review tasks. It spotted a few stray frames, repeated footage, and a misspelled proper name in the subtitles. In the audio and subtitle task (QC2), it correctly located silence and a volume drop but missed the out-of-sync subtitle. It mentioned the suspect subtitle in its report and left it off the issue list because it could not listen to the audio. QC2 scored 80.00 without speed, below the pass threshold.

Revisions

It earned full marks on both tasks with clear revision notes and left the rest of the edit alone. The hardest task (FIX3) had a note with an ambiguous reference. It scored 75.00 without speed, below the pass threshold. It left the configuration alone, listed candidate clips, and asked the lead director to confirm. Its picks differed from the candidates the task was designed around.

Subtitles

It passed both subtitle tasks that provided a transcript. In the original-audio subtitle task (SUB3), it reported that it was blocked and submitted no subtitle file. It could not listen to the audio and declined to copy the supplied reference transcript. The task scored 0.

Disclaimer

One shot. Each task was run once, with no averaging across runs. Scores are for reference only.

The benchmark covers 8 roles and 22 scored tasks drawn from one creator's editing workflow. It does not measure general ability. Results reflect the access route, reasoning effort, tool setup, and model version used during testing in November 2026. Timing also depends on network conditions and provider load at the time. This project sets its own scoring rules and reviews some tasks by hand. See the notes next to flagged results.

The tasks and answer keys are private. This is a personal project with no affiliation to model providers. It offers no advice on which model to choose.
